Mark points a pupil can be half-way on, and results the teacher can actually read

Three things came out of running the 30-pupil sim against the new card.

1. A question with ONE mark point cannot produce an amber - the pupil either hit it or
   they did not - so a whole class came back all green and all red with nothing in
   between, and the card had nothing to say about anyone. On the run that exposed it the
   writer had ignored "2 or 3 points" and returned 2/1/1. If the first draft is thin we
   now ask once more, against the questions we already have, and keep the better draft.

2. The writer bundles two ideas into one string and drops the capital after the comma,
   so "explains that the denominators must be the same,explains that the numerators must
   be the same" arrived as a single point - which cannot be half hit, which is the whole
   reason the marking counts points at all. splitPoints now breaks on any full stop,
   semicolon or spaced dash, on the ", the pupil" join, and on a comma followed by the
   verbs the writer reaches for when it bundles.

3. /api/results demanded a class key, and a class made from a teacher account has none -
   so the endpoint refused the teacher who owned the class and every read-back came back
   empty. Three failing checks in the sim were that one bug. A class made without an
   account still needs its key.

30 pupils, 3 questions, real AI on every turn: 18/30 graded to the band a teacher would
give, and the safety checks clean - no pupil two bands out, nobody who clearly understood
was failed, nobody who gave nothing was passed. That is honest, not good: the marking
still disagrees with a teacher about 4 times in 10. What changed is that the remaining
disagreements are now next-door bands rather than a green pupil marked red.

eval/marks-eval.js checks the points themselves, because they are printed on the card and
so are read by a teacher: 28 of 31 came back as single clean lines.

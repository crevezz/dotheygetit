FAQ

Questions teachers actually ask.

How do I check if students understood a lesson?

Have them explain it back to you in their own words, not answer multiple choice or copy from notes. Get It? does this in two minutes: students have a short AI-led chat about what you just taught, and you get a green/amber/red view of who understood, who's shaky, and who's faking it - for the whole class, with no marking.

How do you know if a student is faking understanding?

The classic tells are an echoed phrase with no explanation, a confident wrong answer, or a shrug and a smile. A quick spoken or typed follow-up question usually flushes it out. Get It? automates this: if a pupil waffles or echoes without explaining, the AI digs deeper before marking them shaky or faking it.

What can I use instead of exit tickets?

Exit tickets work, but the paper pile is what makes teachers quietly stop using them. Get It? keeps the same idea - a quick check at the end of a lesson - but pupils answer on their own phone with no printing, no collecting, and no marking. You get the class result on one screen straight after the lesson.

Is there a free tool to check student understanding?

Yes. Get It? is free during its beta - every teacher and every class gets unlimited checks, with no card required. Students don't need an account either, just the class code you share with them.

How long does it take to set up an understanding check?

Under a minute. You type the topic you taught, the AI writes the questions (which you can edit or delete), and you get a class code to share with pupils straight away.

Do students need to install anything or make an account?

No. Students just need the class code. There's nothing to install and it works on any phone - no app, no login, no email required.
